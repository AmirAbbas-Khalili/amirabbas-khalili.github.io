abstract: |
  With the continuous advancement of science and the growing importance of environmental protection and cost reduction, adopting modern methods in asphalt pavement has become essential in road construction. This paper divides these methods into three main categories: modern construction techniques, advanced recycling methods, and the use of new materials, with a focus on reviewing key examples in each category.

  First, modern construction techniques, such as roller-compacted concrete and the application of nanotechnology, are discussed. New materials and additives, including rubber powder, emulsified bitumen, and stone mastic asphalt (SMA), are then examined. Finally, hot and cold recycling methods are discussed, highlighting their importance in environmental protection and the conservation of natural resources.

  The study emphasizes that not only new materials and construction techniques, but also scientifically developed recycling methods, can improve the physical, chemical, and rheological properties of pavement materials. These approaches can also contribute to national economic efficiency and significantly reduce maintenance and rehabilitation costs.
