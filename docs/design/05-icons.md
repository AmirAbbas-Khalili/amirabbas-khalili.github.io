# Icons

- Phosphor Icons, regular weight, minimal monochrome presentation.
- Use official SVG assets from @phosphor-icons/core. No manually drawn icon paths.
- Keep control icons around 14-18 px. Touch targets stay at least 44 px.
- Icons supplement text or accessible names; do not replace navigation labels with ambiguous pictograms.
- Checkpoint 1: caret, menu, globe, home, outward arrow, close.
- Rollout to other pages occurs after homepage approval.
- Source: https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core
