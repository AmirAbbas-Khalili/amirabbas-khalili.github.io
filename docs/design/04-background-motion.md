## Other pages, later
Align sky blues, daylight, soft atmosphere, and materials with the homepage. Use related abstract/mechanical/architectural treatments or quiet pale surfaces, not the same bridge repeatedly.

## Ambient motion, later checkpoint
User likes one tiny bird crossing or very slow cloud movement, approximately twice per minute.
At most one short sparse event every 30 seconds; avoid prominent loops or UI bobbing.
Must be subtle, low contrast, and out of the reading area. Pause when page hidden and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
No ambient animation is installed in checkpoint 1.
